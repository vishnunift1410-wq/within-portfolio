BUSINESS CHALLENGE

A luxury flagship needs more than an announcement.

WITHIN was launching their first flagship store on MG Road, New Delhi — a multi-floor experience space bringing together furniture, art, craft, and storytelling. The challenge wasn't generating noise. It was generating the right response from the right audience.

The brand's account baseline was ~100–150 likes per post. It needed a campaign that could build genuine pre-launch anticipation, drive footfall from design professionals and architects, and sustain engagement beyond opening day — without losing brand integrity in the process.

OBJECTIVE

Four outcomes to hit.

1
Build curiosity and awareness before doors opened — without revealing everything too early.
2
Create a store opening moment worth noticing — a piece of content that would function as the brand's arrival on MG Road.
3
Generate qualified footfall — architects, designers, and serious buyers, not just casual visitors.
4
Establish the store as a design destination. Not a retail space — a place with a point of view.

STRATEGIC THINKING

The Instagram grid designed as a campaign narrative — not a posting schedule.

The core decision was to map each piece of content to a specific function in the audience's journey. The sequence had to feel intentional — even to someone scrolling without knowing the strategy behind it.

01
BUILD CURIOSITY
March 8–17
Show the making process. Raw construction footage, a principal walking through an unfinished space. Let the audience sense something significant is being built before they know what it is.
02
THE REVEAL
March 22–28
Three AI-rendered exterior visuals designed as a panoramic triptych that reads across all three grid slots. Followed by the chair-on-MG-Road reel — a bold location statement with minimal words.
03
INVITATION + STORY
April 5 onward
Shift from announcement to experience. The Rasmalai reel celebrated the opening through cultural ritual. Then the Sangam editorial — the piece that reframed the campaign's reach entirely.
THE SANGAM BET
Once the store was open, promotional content would lose momentum. I decided to write the Sangam piece from a cultural angle — leading with the story of the Ganga river as a metaphor, before introducing the art collection as an interpretation of that story. This was my initiative. The brief was short and product-focused. I reframed it. The result was the campaign's highest-performing piece — with no paid promotion.

SANGAM — DEEP DIVE

The highest-performing piece led with meaning, not product.

Sangam (P7) wasn't boosted. It didn't run ads. It reached 65,889 accounts — 82.5% of whom had never followed the brand before — purely through organic discovery and private sharing.

BUSINESS IMPACT

What the campaign delivered for WITHIN.

Significant footfall increase at the MG Road flagship post-launch. Architects and design professionals proactively reached out for store visits.
Multiple client enquiries for new collections generated through the campaign period.
Sangam specifically generated direct leads and purchase queries for the limited-edition art collection. 885 bio link taps from one organic reel indicate measurable commercial intent.
Campaign-wide: 2,686 new followers across all 9 pieces — quality audience growth in a relevant creative/design demographic.
82.5% non-follower reach on the Sangam reel means the brand's discovery radius expanded significantly beyond its existing audience.
KEY LEARNING

The highest-performing piece led with meaning, not product. Writing the Sangam copy from the perspective of the river's story — before introducing the collection — created emotional resonance that drove organic virality.

The 3.4% save rate and 1.9% share rate signal the audience found it worth returning to and sharing in personal conversations. That is the difference between content that gets seen and content that gets remembered.
