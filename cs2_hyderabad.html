BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Entering a new city market requires cultural credibility, not just a store announcement.

Hyderabad has a strong design identity — ornament, detail, layered history, and the visual sensibility of its Nizami heritage. A generic "we're open" post wouldn't land.

The brand needed to feel like it belonged there — not as a visitor, but as something that had always shared the city's values. The challenge was to communicate that belonging through content, before the audience had even walked through the door.

OBJECTIVE

Four outcomes for a new market.

1
Build cultural connection with the Hyderabad design audience — not just awareness.
2
Showcase the renovated store spaces through atmospheric, detail-led content.
3
Communicate product texture and luxury through close-up, material-first content.
4
Establish WITHIN as a brand that belongs in Hyderabad's cultural landscape — not just its retail map.

STRATEGIC THINKING

Don't position WITHIN in Hyderabad. Position Hyderabad inside WITHIN.

The city isn't a backdrop for the store. It's evidence that the brand's values — ornament, detail, craftsmanship — are the same values Hyderabad has built its identity on. The brand belongs here not because it opened a store, but because it thinks the same way the city does.

HERO IMAGE STRATEGY

B&W photograph of Charminar with a WITHIN chair in full colour in the foreground. One visual argument: this brand belongs in this city's story. The colour-pop technique creates immediate visual contrast and curiosity against the monochrome backdrop.

GRID AS VISUAL SYSTEM

Designed the complete grid before producing any individual piece. Colour language: mauve/dusty rose + B&W photography + warm wood tones. The grid reads as a complete world, not a series of individual posts.

THE CORE INSIGHT
"Hyderabad has always lived between memory and modernity. That's why WITHIN feels so at home here." — the positioning line that anchored the entire campaign's storytelling direction.

BUSINESS IMPACT

What this campaign delivers for WITHIN in Hyderabad.

Established cultural positioning for WITHIN in the Hyderabad market — the brand enters the city with a point of view, not just an address.
Grid-level design creates a cohesive brand world communicated through a single feed view — the city's design audience sees a brand that speaks their visual language.
Strong early engagement signals across the hero piece and interior content — carousel depth (164 → 62 → 24 → 19) shows sustained audience interest beyond the cover image.
Demonstrates the ability to enter a new city market with a culturally intelligent approach — replicable as the brand expands to other cities.
KEY LEARNING

Designing the grid as a system first — before producing any individual piece — ensures every post contributes to a larger argument rather than standing alone.

The coherence that results is felt by the audience even when they can't name it. It's the difference between a brand's page and a brand's world.
